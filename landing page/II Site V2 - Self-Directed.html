The Memo

Every position begins with a memo.

A sealed record of the reasoning behind a position: classification, sizing, confidence, where the idea came from, and the case for it.

Once sealed, it locks. Editing it creates a new version. The original is never overwritten.

You will read it later and disagree with yourself. That is the record working.

The Process
01

Write

The first time, you fill out the memo: classification, sizing, confidence, idea origin, and the investment case.

02

Re-underwrite

As the thesis is tested, you revise it section by section. Each revision is a new version. The original is never overwritten.

03

Capital Decision

The decision to initiate, increase, or reduce follows the memo. Not the other way around.

A memo is not written once.

Accountability

Every dollar is benchmarked. Every forecast is scored.

Benchmark

Every position, measured against the S&P 500, dollar for dollar. The gap is shown plainly, including when negative.

Calibration

Stated confidence, scored against what happened. Most active investors are overconfident.

Active Positions

What you hold, ordered by weight. Each position is open, measured, and accountable to its memo.
