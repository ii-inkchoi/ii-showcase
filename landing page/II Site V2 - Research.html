/01Premise

Serious investors analyze businesses.
Everyone else trades on price.

Most retail platforms surface price, news, and social sentiment. These are the easiest signals to display and the least useful for capital allocation.

This platform inverts that. The research surface defaults to multi-year financial history, capital allocation patterns, and the business behind the ticker.

/02Fiscal.ai Max

Professional-grade research.
Included.

Every membership includes Fiscal.ai Max, normally $99/month. Multi-year income statements, balance sheets, cash flow statements. Return on capital. Free cash flow. Margin trends. Capital allocation history. Clean comparisons across companies and time.

/03Bring your own AI

Use the AI you already trust.

The system provides structured research prompts. Copy them. Paste them into Claude, ChatGPT, or Perplexity. Bring the analysis back to the memo.

This is intentional. The AI you choose is the AI you're accountable to. The platform does not become a black box of inferred recommendations. The decision remains yours.

/05Doctrine

Research is a discipline.
Not a feature.

Trading apps treat research as a tab you visit once before clicking buy. This platform treats research as the workflow that produces the memo. The two are inseparable.

If the research isn't done, the memo can't be sealed. If the memo can't be sealed, the position doesn't open. The discipline holds.
