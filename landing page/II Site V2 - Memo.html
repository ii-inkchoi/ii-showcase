The Memo

One document. Sealed before capital moves. Immutable once committed. Every Self-Directed position is held against an investment memo that cannot be revised in hindsight.

/01Premise

The hardest discipline is committing to a thesis before the market tells you whether it was right.

Most investors form a thesis loosely — a feeling, a hot tip, a chart pattern. Then they buy. Months later, they remember the thesis differently. The losing call was always tentative. The winning call was always conviction.

Hindsight rewrites memory. The Memo is the mechanism that prevents this. The thesis you wrote is the thesis you owned.

/02Anatomy

Five components.
One sealed document.

The Memo is structured. Not free-form. Each component forces a specific kind of thinking before capital moves.

/03Surface
The Memo · Live
ATZ · Aritzia Inc. · Sealed memo · Thesis, kill criteria, intrinsic value, margin of safety
Fig. 01 The Memo in its Live state.

Every Self-Directed position is held against a sealed investment memo. Immutable once sealed. Each revision creates a new version (V2, V3, etc.). Prior versions stay on the record.

Inside the memo:

  • Classification of the investment type
  • Stated position size as a fraction of the portfolio
  • Full investment case with kill criteria
  • Confidence calibration with time horizon
Underwrite with the AI you already use — Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity. The system provides the protocol. The decision is yours.
/04Lifecycle

A memo lives. It doesn’t sit still.

Once sealed, a memo enters one of five states. The system tracks the state and surfaces appropriate prompts. Triggered memos require attention. Re-underwriting creates a new version on the record.

Draft Composing the memo. Position not yet opened. No capital committed. Pre-seal
Live Memo sealed, position opened. Thesis tracking against reality. The honest test begins. Committed
Triggered A kill criterion has been met. The system surfaces the prompt. Re-underwrite or close. Decision required
Stale Time elapsed without scheduled review. Thesis may still hold — or may not. Confirm or revise. Review window
Closed Position exited. Memo retained on the record. Outcome compared to stated confidence. Calibration updated. Archived
/05Open Questions

A thesis doesn’t get to sit still.

When events cross your kill criteria. When materially new information arrives. When time elapses without review. The system surfaces a decision prompt.

Choosing not to decide is a decision. It’s on the record.

Big MoveMaterial price movement against the position. Reassess whether thesis still holds.
Material MoveNew filing, earnings event, or capital allocation decision. Note the change.
Kill Criteria triggeredA condition you sealed into the memo has been hit. Re-underwrite or close.
Thesis ReviewScheduled review window. Confirm conviction or revise.
Memo PendingPosition opened without sealed thesis. The system requires one.
/06Doctrine

The memo is the receipt.
The portfolio is the consequence.

In most platforms, positions exist without explanation. Closed positions disappear entirely. Memory is selective.

Here, every position carries its origin document. Every closed position carries its post-mortem. The record is permanent.

This is not paperwork. It is the difference between investing and trading. You either committed to a thesis, or you didn’t. The memo decides.
