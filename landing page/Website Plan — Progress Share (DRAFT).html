01

What this is

The marketing website for Intelligent Investing — a self‑directed investor platform built on one idea: judgment must be recorded before it can be improved.

Brand direction: severe, institutional, monochrome. Bloomberg / Palantir tone — not Robinhood / Wealthsimple. The site reads as serious infrastructure, not a consumer fintech app.

Purpose: explain the thesis · present the two tracks (Managed, Self‑Directed) + research · build credibility (regulatory, evidence, manifesto) · convert to app / membership.
