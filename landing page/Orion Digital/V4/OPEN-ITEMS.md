## Questions the mockup leaves open

1. **Footnote 2 is printed twice for different things.** The mockup marks both Cash &
   Investments and Adjusted EV with a 2, and its footnote list defines 2 as "Adjusted EV is a
   non-IFRS measure." One of the two references is wrong.
2. **The outlook date reads July 30, 2025** for a fiscal 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance.
3. **Cash and investments.** V1's composition was C$23.3M cash, C$1.8M restricted cash, C$4.2M
   marketable securities and C$5.6M investments, which sums to C$34.9M against the C$35M shown.
4. **Adjusted EV wording.** The definition carried in V1 was Greg's conceptual version and was
   never reviewed by securities counsel. The mockup prints no definition at all.
5. **The tooltips.** The mockup shows an info marker on Cash & Investments, ownership, shares
   retired and Adjusted EV. What they open is not in the image.
6. **The row expanders.** Each Transition row has a plus in the mockup. What it opens is not
   in the image, so the control is not on the page; it comes back when the panels are
   specified, not before.
7. **The balance sheet.** V3 argued the correction on the page, before the market number. The
   mockup makes it a link. That is a change of argument, not of layout.
8. **Compliance.** The mockup has no forward-looking or market-data language and no
   holding-company paragraph. The live site carries the last of those. Nothing on this page has
   been through securities counsel.
