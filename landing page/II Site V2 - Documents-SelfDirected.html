Self-Directed

Legal structure, fees, execution, and operational disclosures for the Self-Directed track of Intelligent Investing.
Operating entityIntelligentInvesting Securities Inc.
Last updatedJune 8, 2026

Overview

The Self-Directed track allows members to allocate capital directly, with the discipline of The Memo, the accountability of the S&P 500 benchmark, and the research environment of Fiscal.ai Max.

This document describes the operating entity, execution mechanics, fees, FX handling, custody, and regulatory framework for Self-Directed accounts.

All investment decisions in Self-Directed accounts are the member’s responsibility. No suitability review is conducted. No advice is provided. The Memo, Benchmark, and Calibration features are accountability tools, not recommendations.

Operating Entity

Self-Directed accounts are opened with IntelligentInvesting Securities Inc., an investment dealer regulated by the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO).

The entity is a subsidiary of Orion Digital Corp.

Trading & Execution

Supported Markets

Canadian and US listed equities. ETFs supported on both markets. Mutual funds, options, and crypto are not currently supported.

Account Types

  • TFSA
  • RRSP
  • Non-Registered

Order Execution

All orders are routed to and executed by third-party market access providers. Execution venue and best-execution policies are described in the Self-Directed Terms of Use.

Fees & Charges

Membership Fee

$20 per month. Covers access to the Intelligent Investing platform across both tracks (Self-Directed and Managed).

Trading Commissions

None. No per-trade commissions on Canadian or US listed equities and ETFs.

Other

  • No account maintenance fees
  • No inactivity fees
  • No exit fees
  • Regulatory fees (e.g., SEC fees on US sales) passed through at cost without markup

Foreign Exchange

Trades involving US securities may require FX conversion between CAD and USD. The following policies apply:

  • Intelligent Investing does not charge an FX fee
  • Intelligent Investing does not apply an FX markup
  • FX is executed at the rate displayed by a third-party FX partner
  • In certain circumstances, Intelligent Investing may incur a gain or loss on the FX transaction. This does not increase the FX rate paid by members.
  • FX is not contingent on transaction volume or frequency and does not incentivize increased trading activity

Custody

Self-Directed account assets are held with CI Investment Services Inc., an independent custodian that is a CIRO member.

Accounts are protected by the Canadian Investor Protection Fund (CIPF) in accordance with CIPF coverage policy.

Order Types

Limit Order (default)

The default order type in Self-Directed is Limit. Market orders require explicit selection. This reverses the market standard, and is intentional: limit orders preserve price discipline.

Guardrails

  • Limit prices outside ±10% of the current market are flagged for confirmation
  • Good-Til-Canceled (GTC) orders expire automatically after 90 days to prevent forgotten orders from executing in unexpected market conditions
  • A 1-second "Deliberate Proceed" hold is applied before execution on each order

Regulatory Status

IntelligentInvesting Securities Inc. is an investment dealer regulated by CIRO. The entity is a CIPF member.

Self-Directed accounts are operated under execution-only rules. No suitability assessment is conducted on individual trades. No advice is provided.

Orion Digital Corp. is the parent company and is not itself an investment adviser or dealer.

Agreements

By opening a Self-Directed account, you enter into the following agreements:

  • Self-Directed Terms of Use
  • Account-type specific declarations (Declaration of Trust for TFSA/RRSP)
  • W-8BEN or W-9 form for US securities trading (depending on citizenship)
  • Custody Agreement (with CI Investment Services Inc.)

Each agreement is presented during account opening. Copies are available in-app and on request from support@intelligentinvesting.ai.
