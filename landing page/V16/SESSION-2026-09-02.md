## Two rules added to TITLE-TIERS

### 1. A fixed-width element sharing a row with prose

> **Prose width is the rule value. A narrowing row does not shrink the prose — the fixed-width
> element takes its own line, and left-aligns there.**

Found via `.trust9`: `.tl` is declared in `u` so it tracks the window, `.regmarks` is pixel-fixed
(logo heights are px) and carried `flex:none`, so every pixel lost came off the text. Measured at
1512 / 1280 / 1100 / 1000 / 901 the lead ran **2 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 lines** while the marks stayed
391px. Managed was worse — its mark row is 421px, so at 1000 the marks *overlapped* the lead.

`flex:0 0 <6col>` is load-bearing. With a content-sized or `0` basis the prose either eats the free
space or gets squeezed, and flex decides wrapping **before** shrinking, so a content basis wraps the
marks while room is still there.

### 2. Caption width

> **A caption takes the width of the thing it annotates. Do not pin it to a column count** — a
> `max-width` number is a fossil of "how many columns was the target back then".
> **A running note that flows with body text is 6 col.**
> Multiple caption lines under one target: `margin` **0** between them, leading only; target → first
> caption **24px**.

Verified across 7 existing captions (they already obeyed it). The two that broke were the ones whose
target had moved: the constellation caption (`528.3u`, from when the figure was 4 col) and managed's
table footnotes (`807u`, from when they sat under 6-col prose).

The 6-col half was set by her explicitly after a 12-col trial: at 12 col a 9px mono line runs ~190
characters and stops being readable.

## The one bug behind most of it: the rail gutter

The rail is 1645u = 12 tracks of 109.583u with **30u** between. Almost every row was using **28u**
(stat rows 42u) as its column-gap. 28u and 42u are vertical spacing-scale values; used as gutters
they put cells 2 and 3 a few u off the column lines. Invisible until measured, which is why it
survived this long. Fixed on all three pages:

- `.refuse` · `.tri` · `.acct3` · `.modes` · `.dstats` · `.cstrip` → **1-4 / 5-8 / 9-12**
- `.devgrid` · `.close9 .cols` → **1-6 / 7-12** (also moved off a hardcoded 807u onto the formula)
- `.rows3 .row` → **1 / 2-12**
- memo · calibration · constellation figure tracks → 5 col to **4 col (9-12)**

Codified in `shared/TITLE-TIERS.md` as **GRID GUTTER**, with the n-column formula and the check:
`(x - railLeft) / railWidth * 12 + 1`. A correct 3-up reads `1-4.85 | 5.07-8.93 | 9.15-13` — the
fractions are the gutters, not error.

## Figure height cannot come from aspect-ratio

The closing constellation plate had to end on the same line as the white CTA beside it. The gap was
**149 / 119 / 52 / 45 px** at 1440 / 1512 / 1920 / 2200 — it moves because the plate scales with
`--u` and the left column is text, which does not. No single ratio can hold that. The height is now
derived from the row (`align-self:stretch` + `height:100%` + `aspect-ratio:auto`, caption absolute
below). Difference is **0px at all four widths**. Codified as **FIGURE HEIGHT**.

The constellation drawing is bound to width (`R = W*0.40`), so the taller plate does not stretch it.

### 2026-09-04 known-work 8: one prose measure

Not two spellings - two designs. `--prose` was a hardcoded length (807.5u, stepped to 947.08u);
`--prose-cols` was a count fed through an inline formula whose gutter was `--gutter`
(clamp(16px,1.5vw,24px)), not the rail 30u. They agreed to within a pixel only because at 6 of 12
columns the gutter mostly cancels: (100% + g)/12*6 - g == 50% - g/2.

Now one source. `--prose-cols` is the count; `--prose` derives from the rail:

  --prose: calc((1645 * var(--u) + 30 * var(--u)) / 12 * var(--prose-cols) - 30 * var(--u))

807.5u at 6, 947.08u at 7 - the same numbers, no magic constant, and the <=1799 step is now one
declaration (`--prose-cols:7`) instead of a second length. All call sites read `var(--prose)`.

**--prose must be a LENGTH, not a percentage.** The first attempt used the percentage form of the
same formula. A percentage resolves against each element own containing block, and `--prose` is
applied inside boxes narrower than the rail: a 602px `.grp` capped its body at **290px**, `.pricehd9`
went 538 -> 258, and managed grew **374px**. Caught by comparing page height against the recorded
baseline, reverted from `_archive/*-pre-prose-20260904.html`, redone as a length.

Verified: index 9,512 and managed 9,969 at 1920, both **identical to the pre-change baseline**.
Pricing / newsroom / manifesto prose moved 602 -> 609px - intended, they now measure the same six
rail columns as the other pages.
