INTELLIGENT INVESTING · A CAPITAL ALLOCATION SYSTEM
THE MANIFESTO

We are not here to make investing feel easy.

Fifteen entries. The doctrine of Intelligent Investing in full.
Read in sequence, or by entry number.
NO. 001

A statement of position

We are not here to make investing feel easy.

Easy is what produces delusion. Delusion is what produces error.

Intelligent Investing exists to remove illusion. Not to replace it with a better story.

NO. 002

The shift

Investing is not trading. It is capital allocation.

The difference is not semantic. It is the difference between reacting and deciding.

NO. 003

What this is

Intelligent Investing is a discipline-first capital allocation system. Built so that judgment can compound over decades.

It is not a tool. It is a system you operate inside.

  • Reduce behavioral error.
  • Enforce long-term thinking.
  • Slow decision-making where speed destroys outcomes.
  • Align structure with discipline.
NO. 004

The system

This is not a collection of features. It is a structured environment for allocating capital deliberately.

Inside it:

  • A disciplined core approach.
  • A structured decision process.
  • A professional research environment.
  • Behavioral design that enforces discipline.

Each element exists for one reason: to support long-term discipline.

NO. 005

How it works

Capital can be allocated in different ways within the system. You make every decision.

A broad, low-cost, market-based approach is available as a starting point you can choose, an asset-allocation option, not a recommendation, because few who set out to do better than the market can explain, in advance, why they expect to.

Active positions can be built alongside it. Treated as a craft, not a shortcut.

Every decision is expected to be:

  • Thought through.
  • Documented.
  • Understood in terms of risk.
NO. 006

The environment you are operating in

Markets are not fair.

  • Narrative.
  • Incentives.
  • Overconfidence.
  • Short-term feedback loops.

Most participants are reacting. A small minority is allocating.

This system is built for the latter.

NO. 007

Core system principles

Discipline over prediction.
Outcomes improve when error is reduced, not when forecasts multiply.

Structure over willpower.
Good intentions fail without constraints.

Time over urgency.
Compounding requires duration most cannot tolerate.

Friction as intelligence.
If a decision feels too easy, it usually is.

NO. 008

The behavioral layer

Most poor results are not analytical. They are behavioural.

This system is built to help surface:

  • Overconfidence.
  • Impatience.
  • Narrative chasing.
  • Inconsistent decision-making.

And to apply pressure in the opposite direction. Not through motivation. Through design.

NO. 009

Research & analysis

Every membership includes access to Fiscal.ai, a professional-grade research environment.

Most investors act on price and headlines. Serious investors study businesses.

The tools inform your analysis. They do not give advice and do not recommend securities. Every decision is yours.

NO. 010

Compounding

Compounding is not driven by activity. It is what remains after disciplined capital allocation is sustained across years, not quarters.

Few investors can maintain that discipline. This system exists to make it possible.

NO. 011

Mastery over moments

This system is built around long arcs, not short wins.

Learning compounds. Judgment compounds. Character compounds.

The investor improves. The record follows.

NO. 012

Measurement

You cannot improve a decision you never measure.

The system shows you how your choices compare against a broad market reference. We use the S&P 500 as one such reference, not because it is the only benchmark, nor the right one for every goal, but because for long-term equity investing it remains one of the most widely used standards in the world.

The point is not to beat it. The point is to know where you stand.

Measurement improves decisions. Decisions are the part you control.

NO. 013

Who this is for

People who:

  • Think in decades.
  • Distrust easy answers.
  • Value restraint.
  • Prefer being right over being entertained.
NO. 014

Who this is not for

Not for what you are. For what you are looking for.

If you are here for:

  • Excitement.
  • Shortcuts.
  • Reassurance.
  • A market to blame.

this is not the place.

The standards are high. The door is open.

If you value clarity, welcome.

NO. 015

Aligned incentives

Membership $20 / month. Cancel anytime.

There are no trading commissions on self-directed accounts. Our core business is the subscription, and what sustains it is your reason to stay.

On currency: unlike many brokers, we do not charge a separate foreign-exchange fee. We may earn a small spread built into the conversion rate, so it is not free, but there is no surcharge added on top. On managed accounts, we charge a management fee of 0.10%.

We try and optimize for one thing above all: whether the system makes you a better allocator over time.

We are not building an investing app.
We are building the operating system for capital allocation.
