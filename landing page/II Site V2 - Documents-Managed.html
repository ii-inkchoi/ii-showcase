Managed

Legal structure, fees, and operational disclosures for the Managed track of Intelligent Investing.
Operating entityIntelligentInvesting Wealth Management Inc.
Last updatedJune 8, 2026

Overview

The Managed track of Intelligent Investing provides supervised portfolio management for members who prefer that capital allocation be handled by Mogo on their behalf, within the same disciplined framework as Self-Directed.

This document describes the operating structure, fees, custody, portfolio construction approach, and tax handling that govern Managed accounts.

This is a plain-language summary. For binding terms, refer to the Managed Account Agreement (linked in §Agreements below).

Operating Entity

Managed accounts are opened with IntelligentInvesting Wealth Management Inc., a registered portfolio manager. The entity is a subsidiary of Orion Digital Corp.

The portfolio manager has discretionary authority to allocate capital within the parameters established by your Investor Profile (calibrated during sign-up and updated periodically).

Fees & Charges

Management Fee

0.10% per annum, calculated on the average daily balance of assets in your Managed portfolio. Applied monthly. Lower than virtually all alternatives in the Canadian market.

Membership Fee

$20 per month. Covers access to the Intelligent Investing platform across both tracks (Managed and Self-Directed).

Other

  • No commissions on trades
  • No FX markup on currency conversions
  • No performance fees
  • No account maintenance fees
  • No exit fees

Custody

Managed account assets are held with CI Investment Services Inc., an independent custodian that is a CIRO member.

Custody is separate from the portfolio manager. CI Investment Services Inc. holds, settles, and reports on your assets. Mogo allocates within them. The separation is intentional and required by regulation.

Accounts are protected by the Canadian Investor Protection Fund (CIPF) in accordance with CIPF coverage policy.

Portfolio Construction

Managed portfolios are constructed around two core principles:

  • Diversified, market-based allocation as the disciplined default
  • Long-duration positioning aligned with compounding, not activity

The portfolio is rebalanced periodically. Trades are executed to maintain target allocations. The portfolio manager does not attempt to time markets or chase short-term performance.

Tax Handling

Managed accounts support TFSA, RRSP, and Non-Registered account types. Tax slips are issued annually in accordance with CRA requirements.

Capital gains and losses are realized as part of normal portfolio rebalancing. Tax-loss harvesting may be applied where appropriate within the account structure.

Regulatory Status

IntelligentInvesting Wealth Management Inc. is a registered portfolio manager in Canada. The entity is regulated by the relevant provincial securities commissions.

Orion Digital Corp. is the parent company and is not itself an investment adviser or dealer.

Agreements

By opening a Managed account, you enter into the following agreements:

  • Managed Terms of Use
  • Investment Management Agreement
  • Custody Agreement (with CI Investment Services Inc.)
  • Account-type specific declarations (Declaration of Trust for TFSA/RRSP)

Each agreement is presented during account opening. Copies are available in-app and on request from support@intelligentinvesting.ai.
