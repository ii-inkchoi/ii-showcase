No. 01The Record

Most products record transactions.
Intelligent Investing records judgment.

An investor can execute every trade correctly, yet not explain the portfolio.

Most platforms solved the execution layer. The judgment layer, the framework for recording decisions, measuring them against a benchmark, and learning from outcomes, was left to the investor to figure out alone.

Intelligent Investing exists because it was not.

Every position carries its reasoning: classification, sizing, confidence, origin, and the full investment case, recorded the moment capital is committed and re-underwritten on schedule. The portfolio is the artifact. The record is the product.
