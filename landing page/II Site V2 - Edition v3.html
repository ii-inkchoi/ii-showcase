No. 01Reality

Very few systems record judgment.

An investor can execute every trade correctly and still build a portfolio they cannot explain.

Most platforms solved the execution layer. The judgment layer, the framework for recording decisions, measuring them against a benchmark, and learning from outcomes, was left to the investor to figure out alone.

Intelligent Investing exists because it was not.

No. 02The Allocator

Most people own positions.
Allocators own decisions.

An investor owns positions. An allocator owns the reasoning behind them, and stays accountable to it over time. The objective is not activity. It is capability. One model underneath every decision. The last step sharpens the next.
