About

Published by Mogo Inc. and Orion Digital Corp. A platform built for investors who refuse to confuse activity with progress.
/01Position

Intelligent Investing was built
by people tired of platforms that
reward delusion.

The investing industry has spent two decades optimizing for engagement. More trades. More alerts. More noise. The customer’s portfolio is rarely the primary metric.

Mogo Inc. and Orion Digital Corp. built Intelligent Investing as the opposite: a discipline-first capital allocation system designed for compounding over decades, not engagement over weeks.

Our metric is not your activity. It is whether you remained disciplined. Whether you wrote down your thinking. Whether your calibration improved. Whether you stayed long enough for compounding to do its work.
