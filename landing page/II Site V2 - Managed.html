How It Works
01

Set your contribution

Choose a weekly amount. It withdraws automatically on schedule. The amount can be changed or paused at any time.

02

Choose your portfolio

Select from five risk profiles. From full equity to capital preservation. The allocation is managed automatically. You set the risk level once.

03

Weekly, automatically

Contributions run on schedule regardless of market conditions. That consistency is the point, not a feature.

The default is the market. Managed makes that the practice.

Why This Compounds

The most costly investing mistake most people make is not choosing the wrong stock. It is selling during a downturn, pausing contributions when conviction weakens, or waiting for the right moment to start.

Removing the decision removes the moment of failure. Weekly contributions accumulate through market cycles without requiring the investor to make a correct call at the wrong time.

Managed is not passive. It is disciplined. The discipline is in the consistency, not the strategy.
