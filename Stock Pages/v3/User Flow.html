Stock Page · User Flow · V3

Three entry routes converge on the Stock Page. Stock Page branches by persona (first-time vs returning). Both personas surface Fiscal AI, the Research Prompt, and four external AI tools directly on the page — no separate research workspace. Memo is a single free-form textarea. Re-seal loops back to the Stock Page.

V3 simplification (2026-05-26). Removed in V3: 9-step memo creation flow, AI Research v2 page, 8-field structured memo, Pressure Test drawer.

Stock Page
03 · Page Stock Page Branches by persona below — first-time (no memo) vs returning (memo Live).
First-time user Stock Page (First-time) No position, no memo. About company + empty Memo entry + Fiscal AI + Research Prompt + 4 AI tools. Returning user Stock Page (Memo Live) Position held. Memo block (preview + Read More + Re-underwrite) + Fiscal AI + Research Prompt + 4 AI tools.
On-Page Surfaces
Data · drill-in Fiscal AI · ATZ Dedicated workspace. Filings, financials, live fundamentals. Bloomberg-style density.
Inline action · Copy Research Prompt · ATZ Tailored to ATZ at 20% hurdle. 9 output sections, 2,500–3,500 words. Tap the copy icon to copy, then paste anywhere.
External · 4 links AI Tools Claude · ChatGPT · Gemini · Perplexity. Open, paste prompt, get the research document.
The Memo
Editor · Single textarea The Memo Free-form prose. Paste AI response or write your own. Auto-save to draft. Seal Memo commits draft → live. First lines surface as preview on Stock Page.
↺ Seal Memo → back to Stock Page (memo Live, preview reflects first paragraph)
Memo states: Draft (unsealed edits) · Live (sealed, content matches) · Sealed empty (user cleared). Stock Page only reflects the sealed (Live) version. Editing creates a Draft that doesn't propagate until Re-seal.
