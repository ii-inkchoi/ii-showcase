Stock Page + Memo · V3 Handoff

V3 simplification of Stock Page and The Memo. Memo is now a single free-form textarea. Stock Page surfaces Fiscal AI, the Research Prompt, and the four AI tools directly — no separate research workspace.

Designer · Inkyung · Updated May 26, 2026

01 What Changed in V3

CEO direction from the 2026-05-26 review. The V2 structured memo (eight fields, 9-step creation flow) is retired. V1 ships the minimum viable shape, then we add back complexity as user behavior demands it.

Memo is a single textarea
No more Classification / Thesis / Kill / IV / MoS / Probability fields. One free-form prose box. Paste the full AI response or write your own. The first paragraph surfaces as a preview on the Stock Page.
Stock Page absorbs research
No separate AI Research page. Fiscal AI link + Research Prompt card + four AI tool deep-links (Claude · ChatGPT · Gemini · Perplexity) live directly on the Stock Page.
Draft / Live separation
Editing the memo writes to a draft. Stock Page keeps showing the previously sealed version until the user clicks Re-seal Memo. Draft and Live are stored under separate localStorage keys.
Capital Decision → Classification
No intro pages. Capital Decision footer leads straight to classification. Removed legacy stepper pages.
Return Hurdle as first-class concept
Hurdle ladder is 15% / 20% / 25% / 30% / 50%+. The Research Prompt is filled with the active hurdle so the AI's output is tailored.
