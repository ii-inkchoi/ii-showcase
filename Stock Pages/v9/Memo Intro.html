01Why This Exists

Most investors spend their time deciding what to buy. Few spend time deciding why.

As a result, decisions are often driven by narratives, recent price movements, emotions, or hindsight.

The underwriting system exists to make your reasoning explicit before capital is committed.

The objective is not prediction. The objective is improving judgment.

Every underwriting record becomes part of your investing history. Over time, these records reveal the quality of your decision-making.

03Research First

The underwriting system assumes you understand the business. Use whatever process works best for you.

Available tools include:

  • Fiscal.ai Pro
  • AI Research Tools
  • Company Filings
  • Earnings Calls
  • Investor Presentations
  • Industry Research

The quality of the underwriting record is determined by the quality of the research.

The system does not prescribe how you research. It requires that you can defend your conclusions.

04Thesis

Every investment begins with a thesis. A thesis is the reason capital is being allocated.

It should explain:

  • What makes the business attractive
  • Why the opportunity exists
  • Why the market may be mispricing it
  • What must happen for the investment to succeed

Without a thesis there is nothing to test.

The thesis is the foundation of the underwriting record.

05Kill Criteria

Most investors decide when to buy. Few decide when they are wrong.

Kill criteria identify the evidence that would invalidate the thesis.

Examples may include:

  • Deteriorating competitive advantages
  • Failed execution
  • Changes in industry structure
  • Broken capital allocation
  • Incorrect assumptions

The objective is not conviction. The objective is disciplined conviction.

When the evidence changes, the underwriting should change.

06Intrinsic Value

Price tells you what the market believes today.

Intrinsic value represents what you believe the business is worth.

The underwriting process asks you to estimate value before making a capital decision.

The purpose is not precision. The purpose is independent thinking.

Investors who never estimate value become dependent on market prices to tell them what something is worth.

07Margin of Safety

Valuation is uncertain. Every estimate contains error. The margin of safety acknowledges that uncertainty.

It measures the difference between your estimate of value and the current market price.

Generally:

  • Higher uncertainty requires a larger margin of safety.
  • Lower uncertainty may justify a smaller margin of safety.

The goal is not certainty. The goal is protecting against mistakes.

08Probability of a 20% Return

The system asks: What is the probability this investment can compound at 20% annually?

This is not a prediction. It is a calibration exercise.

A 20% annual return is an extraordinarily high hurdle. Very few businesses achieve it. Very few investors achieve it.

The purpose is to force honest assessment of upside potential and improve calibration over time.

As your investing history grows, you will be able to compare your expectations with actual outcomes.

09Research Supports Underwriting

The underwriting system does not prescribe a specific research process.

  • Some investors read annual reports.
  • Some build models.
  • Some study industry structure.
  • Some use Fiscal.ai.
  • Some use AI tools.
  • Most use a combination of approaches.

The objective is not following a checklist.

The objective is gathering enough evidence to complete the underwriting.

Research supports the memo.

The memo supports the decision.

10Capital Decisions

Once underwriting is complete, you can make a capital decision.

This includes:

  • Position role
  • Position sizing
  • Time horizon
  • Probability assessment
  • Intrinsic value
  • Margin of safety

The goal is not certainty. The goal is explicit reasoning.

Every decision should be explainable.

11Re-Underwriting

Markets change. Businesses change. Your understanding changes.

The underwriting process is not a one-time exercise.

Review and update your underwriting when:

  • New information emerges
  • The thesis changes
  • The business changes
  • The price materially changes
  • Your estimate of value changes

The best investors continuously re-underwrite their positions.

12The Record

Every underwriting record becomes part of your investing history.

Over time, these records reveal:

  • Calibration
  • Discipline
  • Thesis quality
  • Decision quality
  • Holding discipline
  • Position sizing discipline

The objective is not to be right every time.

The objective is to become a better capital allocator.
