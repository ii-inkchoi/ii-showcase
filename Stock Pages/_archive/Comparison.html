Same chrome, different body. Both pages share the header, title block, severe palette, and fixed-bottom Capital Decision. What changes is the section stack: the first-time page invites a memo and frames the company, while the recurring page surfaces thesis status, holdings, and performance. The decision destination is identical because the act of committing capital is identical — only the lead-up differs.

Thesis status uses color, not ornament. The Kill Line status indicator counts active invalidation triggers (0 / 1 / 2 of 3 firing) and shifts color across three states: c-700 (intact, quiet), c-400 (approaching, audible), white (invalidated, decision-forcing). No icons, no red, no banners. The same page reads differently because the same words sit in different colors — the doctrine signal travels through type weight alone.
