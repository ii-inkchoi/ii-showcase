The memo is your record — what you believe, why, what would prove you wrong. It ends in one word: Promote or Pass. After saving, it lives on the stock page.

Every capital decision has a memo. Two kinds, chosen on the next step:

Active Allocation
The workflow below.

You're claiming an edge. You believe this position will outperform. Requires a 9-section memo.

Core / Treasury Allocation
Simpler setup.

No specific bet. Broad market exposure or long-term compounding. Simpler setup: role, weight, notes.
