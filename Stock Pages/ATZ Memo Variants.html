Five lifecycle states. Memos flow through Draft → Armed → (Triggered / Stale) → Closed. The state badge in the status bar reflects where the memo is in its lifecycle. Only Triggered uses an active signal (amber + pulse) to demand user attention. The other four states are static dots in monochrome doctrine — Draft and Stale/Closed use dim c-800 to indicate non-active monitoring; Armed uses c-400 to signal the memo is in its primary operating state.
