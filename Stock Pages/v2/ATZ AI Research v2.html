03What You'll Get 9 sections

The structured answer Claude or ChatGPT will return after you paste the prompt. Tap any section to see what it will contain.

1 Business Overview

Two to three paragraphs covering what the business does and how it makes money, where it sits in its industry, its current scale and growth profile, and the single most important thing to understand about this business. The insight that, if missed, would lead to a wrong analysis.

2 Classification

States one classification: compounder, emerging compounder, situational mispricing, or speculative. Provides the case for this classification in one paragraph, and addresses the strongest argument for the alternative classification an investor might otherwise choose, explaining why this choice is more accurate. Each classification implies an integrated judgment about business quality and how the investor will make money.

3 Thesis & Decomposition

Thesis written as it would appear in the memo — 2 to 3 sentences stating why this position should exist, lead with the reason in declarative form. Identifies the load-bearing assumption — the single claim that, if false, collapses the thesis. Then performs a structural decomposition: breaks the relevant base (revenue, units, customers — whatever the assumption is about) into sub-segments by exposure to the load-bearing risk, with size as percentage of total, growth rate, and risk exposure (high / medium / low) for each.

4 Why Now

Two to three sentences on why the timing is right now — what has changed, or what is about to change, that makes this the right moment rather than six months ago or six months from now. For a compounder, the answer is often "the price has come to me" or "the long-term compounding case is intact and the entry point is reasonable." If no genuine "why now" exists, says so directly. The absence itself is a reason to question the timing.

5 Kill Criteria

Four to six kill criteria the investor should adopt for this position. Not a long menu — the actual list. Each one specifies: the metric or event stated specifically, the exact threshold or condition that triggers it, the action if triggered (Sell, Reduce, or Review within a 5-day window), and one sentence on why this matters for this specific business. Leading indicators prioritized — criteria that would trigger before damage is fully visible in the stock price.

6 Intrinsic Value

Method Selection. First determines the right valuation method for this specific business. Multi-segment with different economics? Sum-of-parts. Embedded financial subsidiary? Value separately. Asset-heavy in ways not reflected in earnings? NAV or asset-based. Transitional margins or cyclical trough? Cash flow methods over normalized period. Single-segment steady-state? DCF or multiples. Departs from sector default when needed.

Assumptions. Every material assumption stated explicitly. Conservative version chosen by default (the one that produces lower IV). Conservative / base / aggressive shown for revenue growth (years 1–3, 4–5, terminal), margin trajectory, capital intensity, discount rate, terminal value method. Tax rate and share count changes for consolidated.

Calculation. Walks through the math. Three scenario valuations — downside / base / upside — with explicit probability weights and per-share values.

Conservative IV. Single per-share number.

Margin of Safety. To conservative IV, and to the explicit downside per-share value. Downside MoS is the honest number.

Sensitivity. The single assumption that would most increase IV if shifted to aggressive.

Where This IV Could Be Wrong. Two or three sentences identifying the most likely reason IV is too high — which assumption, segment, or scenario weight is weakest.

7 Probability of Clearing the Hurdle · Binding

Probabilities at 1, 3, and 5 years against the hurdle. Binding triage. Promote if 3-year ≥ 50% (or 40–50% with plausible path up). Pass otherwise.

8 Where to Dig Deeper

Only if Promote. 3–5 specific original research tasks with assumption affected, direction it pushes, and time estimate.

9 Verify on Fiscal

10–15 fact-checks for Fiscal AI. Figures, segment disclosures, guidance, share counts, debt covenants, insider activity.
