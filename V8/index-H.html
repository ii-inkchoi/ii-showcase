NO. 02 · THE MISSING LAYER
The industry solved access.
The industry solved
access.
It never solved judgment.

Commissions went to zero. Fractional shares arrived. Most public markets now fit in a pocket. Access was the easier problem, and the industry finished it.

Professional investment organisations preserve research, review their decisions, and measure attribution. Mainstream retail brokerage largely stops at the transaction record. The reasoning lives somewhere else: a notebook, a chat thread, a memory that edits, or nowhere at all.

Most of us never noticed, because nothing was built to show it. Intelligent Investing adds the missing layer: the decision before the outcome, the alternative that was available, and the record of what changed.

NO. 03 · THE LOOP

Record. Benchmark.
Review. Calibrate.You run it from day one.

A trade is not the important event. The decision is. Most brokerages preserve the trade; this one begins a record of the decision before the outcome is known.

A trade is not the important event. The decision is. Most brokerages preserve the trade. This one records the decision first.

Before the order, the position is classified, its role and size are set, and you state your probability of clearing the hurdle. The investment case can be added later, and when it is, it carries the date it was written, not the date of the order. The system does not invent a history you did not create.

The system can organise evidence, support research, and preserve the record. It does not make the decision, assign the probability, or absorb the consequence. Those remain yours.

A sound decision can lose. A reckless one can pay off. The outcome alone never tells you which you made, which is why the number goes down first. Every active decision begins with a record. When the allocator returns, the case can change. The history cannot.
