02 · MONO IS THREE SIZES

13 · SECTION LABEL AND CTA
11 · notice, date, regulatory label
9 · figure, mockup and data sub-text
12 is retired as of 2026-07-28. It had no role between 11 and 13. If you see 12 anywhere on a mobile view it is drift.
Separate a section heading from an item title. Titles of items inside a section are all 17; anything opening a section or stream is 22 or above. When that collapses you get a news label rendering larger than a product name. Hero display 32 exists once per page, and section box statements are 27.

Spacing is carried by margins only. A flex gap stacks on top of margin-top, which is the most repeated defect on these pages: a 24 intent rendering as 48, 64 or 80. Set row-gap:0 on vertical flex and grid containers. Hairlines are symmetric, the air above a rule matches the air below.

07 · KNOWN MISMATCHES

NO.02 stat figure does not match NO.04. NO.02 renders 22 / 1.4, NO.04 renders 17 / 1.15. The canon is 17 / 1.15; the .g2stat figure was missed when the tier was corrected.
NO.05 statement uses a portfolio colour. #BEEDFD is --pf-balanced-income, a Managed portfolio identity token, on a self-directed page. Doctrine keeps --pf-* off this surface.
NO.02 VIEW MORE opens nothing. The paragraph it used to reveal is hidden by the mobile copy cut, so only the control is left.
The hero trust line is 8px, under the 9px floor, and the smallest thing in the hero at 0.31× the headline. A customer interview on 2026-07-29 found someone who reached the app without knowing II is a licensed brokerage, which is exactly what this line says. Held as-is for now.
