Opportunity cost is the real result. The index is the alternative that was always available. A gain below it is a loss, whether or not the investor ever learns which one they had.

The system holds a view. To maximize return, the core is managed S&P 500 exposure, accumulated weekly, held for decades, and it stays the core until your own record, over years, shows your active decisions did better. Active skill is built against that standard, deliberately. It is not assumed.

Return is not the only goal. Trading performance for involvement is a legitimate choice. Made with the cost in front of you, it is a decision. Made without it, it is the delusion most investors live in, no idea what their activity costs against the benchmark, convinced they are ahead while they trail it badly.

A brokerage gives you tools and no standard. A robo-advisor gives you a standard and no agency. Here the standard is fixed and the choice is yours. What the system will not permit is not knowing.

NO. 05BENCHMARK
05

THERE IS ONE
BENCHMARK NO ONE
CAN ARGUE WITH.

The S&P 500 is the reference every serious record is measured against, the standard the world uses to decide whether an investor added anything at all. A return means nothing until it is placed beside it.

Most compare against a benchmark they can clear. It is the quietest self-deception in the business. For a Canadian the convenient one is the TSX, a smaller field, easier to lead. The system anchors to the S&P 500 because it is the one that cannot be negotiated down. It will show any benchmark you choose. It defaults to the one the world already agrees on, currency and all.

The comparison is not a report you request. It is on every screen, your capital against the same capital in the index, in dollars, on every position, over the same hold. A gain that trailed it is still a shortfall.
