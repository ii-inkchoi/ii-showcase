## Boxing the charts, the one that looks like a regression and is not

**Every plot now sits in the card's box** — `--c-elev` ground, a `0.5px --c-900` hairline, `12px` padding, square corners. Seven plots on the performance screen plus the Calibration chart on the home screen. The card's chart was already boxed and it was the reference.

**The washes' absolute colour could not be preserved, and this must be recorded or someone will "fix" it back.** Both washes composite darker over black than `--c-elev` itself is, so once the ground is lifted no opacity in range can reach their old values: the required numbers come out negative on five of six channels. ## The currency rule

**Currency is a property of the account, stated once per screen.** The user set it. **Stating a global fact repeatedly makes it look local** — four blocks each saying CAD implies the blocks might differ.

**The once-per-screen statement now sits at the right-hand end of the market clock row, on all four screens.** Three reasons, kept because they are the reusable part:

- The clock, the market flags and the currency are all conditions for reading the screen rather than content of it, so they belong together.
- It uses space that was empty, and stops the currency competing with a heading block for the same baseline.
- Each screen has exactly one `.mkt-strip`, so "once per screen" becomes structural rather than a thing to remember.

It got there in two steps. It was first placed beside each screen's headline, which worked on the performance screen (title left, currency right on one row) but failed on Detail and Closed, whose heading blocks are right-aligned, leaving the currency floating alone on the left. The user spotted that, and it moved to the clock row.

**The performance screen was held out of that move on the grounds that it is 7,340px tall** and the 2026-09-04 meeting had removed a foot-level statement because Aidan could not find it. **It was retired because it did not survive its own numbers.** Measured: the old placement sat 6,502px from Top Contributors, and the clock row is 180px higher, so the move changed a 6,500px problem by under 3% while costing the fourth screen its consistency. Record the actual answer, so it is not re-derived wrongly: if the distance turns out to matter in review, the fix is a second statement near Top Contributors, not a marginally less distant single one.

Applied to the home screen: `Total Value` keeps its `CAD`, four inline tokens on position rows were removed, and no row's alignment moved because that group is right-aligned. **The corollary that pass established: CAD is the inherited fact, so a figure in a different currency is a departure and must name itself locally** — which is why `82.40 USD` stays on two rows. Also worth recording: `Total Value` renders in all three deposit states, so the no-capital states are not left with figures and no currency named. That is load-bearing and breaks if anyone state-gates that block.
