Methodology

Matched-mirror comparison. For each external deposit and in-kind transfer, the same CAD amount is allocated to the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF (VFV) at that date's close. Withdrawals reduce both sides proportionally. Cash held in your account is included in your return. We don't adjust for it.
