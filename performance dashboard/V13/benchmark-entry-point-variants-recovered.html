S&P 500 Benchmark, Entry-Point Variants

Four treatments for the card that opens the performance dashboard from the main dashboard's S&P 500 Benchmark section. All four run the performance dashboard's own chart engine, a seeded generator that marks the line to market step by step and re-centres each year on its stated return, so the shape is real, not drawn. All four are scrubbable: drag a finger across the plot to read any point along it, release to return to the resting state. The first three (Alpha Line, Instrument, Headline) keep a permanent readout box in flow above the chart, stating the current values at rest and the scrubbed values while dragging. The fourth (Reveal) carries no permanent box: the chart labels its own lines at rest, and the readout only appears, as an overlay over the chart, while a finger is down.

The chart carries its own numbers: portfolio, S&P and deposits each label their own line at its endpoint, so nothing below the chart repeats what it already states, and the card is shorter than Instrument or Headline. Touching the chart reveals the same dual-date, three-value readout the other variants show permanently. The cost: the S&P-equivalent and portfolio figures are 11px on the chart rather than typographically prominent, and a viewer who never touches the chart never sees the fuller readout.
