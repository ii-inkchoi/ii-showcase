No. 06The System

Every bad decision begins as a behavior.

Overconfidence, confirmation, anchoring, the reflex to react, these, not the market, do the real damage. The operating system moves every position through one loop, and every station is built to surface one of them.

A bias surfaces at the station built to see it, overconfidence at Calibration, confirmation at the Case, anchoring at Re-underwriting, where it is named and held to the record before it quietly sets the decision. The system does not erase these; nothing does. It makes them visible, so they can be accounted for. Research feeds the case but never decides it; every station is held against the S&P at the center.
