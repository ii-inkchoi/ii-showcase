Trading & Foreign Exchange (FX)

Self-Directed Investing

Trades involving US securities may incur an FX conversion.

Intelligent Investing does not charge an FX fee or apply an FX markup to member transactions.

FX is executed at the rate displayed to members by a third-party partner, and in certain circumstances, Intelligent Investing may incur a gain or a loss on FX transactions.

This does not increase the FX rate paid by members. FX is not contingent on transaction volume or frequency, and does not incentivize increased trading activity. We do not charge trading commissions.
